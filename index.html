02 / CURRENTLY WORKING ON

Work in progress.

These are the questions occupying most of my research time right now.

01

DISSERTATION · STATISTICAL METHODOLOGY

Improved Bandwidth Selection for Local Polynomial Derivative Estimation

I am developing a data-driven global bandwidth selector for estimating derivatives of regression functions with local polynomial methods. The work combines finite-sample risk estimation, numerical optimization, theoretical consistency results, Monte Carlo experiments, and reproducible R implementation.

Nonparametric regressionMISEBandwidth selectionMonte Carlo
02

ADDITIONAL DISSERTATION WORK · LIKELIHOOD THEORY

Convexity and Information in the Marginal Likelihood Correction for Logistic Regression

This work asks what changes when the same joint model for a binary outcome and its predictors is viewed through two equivalent likelihood factorizations. I study the marginal correction connecting them: when it is convex, how it changes statistical information, and why symmetry of the predictor distribution can make its contribution vanish exactly. The project extends the theory toward flexible spline-based regression and uses simulation to investigate the results.

03 / RESEARCH & SELECTED PROJECTS

From theory to application.

A selective view of the problems I have chosen to spend time on — across health, finance, mobility, insurance, and predictive modeling.

Population HealthCKD / NHANES

Earlier detection and risk stratification in chronic kidney disease

Using population health data to study nonlinear relationships, risk classification, discrimination and calibration in CKD, while developing a broader methodological research program around earlier detection and risk stratification.

Health EconomicsAfrica · 2002–2022

Health financing, out-of-pocket costs, and mortality

Research on how public health expenditure, household out-of-pocket spending, governance, and demographic pressure jointly relate to mortality across African countries, using generalized additive mixed models to capture nonlinearities and interactions.

Read the published paper ↗
Urban AnalyticsNYC TLC · Ongoing

Urban mobility through New York City taxi data

An ongoing analysis of NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission trip data examining customer behavior, trip patterns, congestion, airport demand, weather effects, and revenue trends. The broader research agenda explores spatial mobility inequality, emerging transportation patterns, tipping behavior, resilience after disruptions, driver-revenue variability, and taxi trips as an evolving transportation network.

Insurance AnalyticsTravelers · Finalist

Predictive auto-insurance modeling

Built a two-stage framework combining negative-binomial claim-frequency and gamma claim-severity models, with feature engineering, model validation, and business interpretation.

UncertaintyMachine Learning

Conformal prediction

Implemented uncertainty-quantification workflows around neural networks, random forests, and KNN to construct prediction sets and compare predictive behavior beyond point accuracy.
