# Denis Folitse — Personal Website

Source code for my personal academic and professional website.

**Live site:** https://sedem-77.github.io/

I am a PhD Candidate in Statistics at the University of Massachusetts Amherst. My work spans statistical methodology, nonparametric regression, computational statistics, quantitative modeling, finance and financial markets, economics, population health, insurance, urban mobility, and statistical software.

This website brings together my research, selected projects, publications, software, teaching and consulting experience, education, presentations, and recognition. It also provides direct access to my résumé and professional profiles.

## Research and projects

My doctoral research includes work on global bandwidth selection for local polynomial derivative estimation and likelihood theory for binary regression. Selected applied projects span chronic kidney disease risk modeling, health financing, urban mobility, insurance analytics, and uncertainty quantification.

## Software

- **binaryClass** — R software for binary classification and statistical modeling.
- **dpilpr** — research software in development for bandwidth selection in local polynomial derivative estimation.
